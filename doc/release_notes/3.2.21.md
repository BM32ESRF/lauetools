## LaueTools 3.2.21 — Release notes

### ⚠️ Changes in behaviour
- **Python 2 is no longer supported** (`requires-python >= 3.8`, as declared in `pyproject.toml`). The `if sys.version_info.major == 3: ... else: <python 2 imports>` branches are removed from 46 modules.
- **plotmeshGUI: the vertical axis of the maps is always the y motor** (`yech`, `yps` or `sy`), whatever the order of the motors in the scan command: the map is transposed when needed. The mouse over the map gives the image index and the value of the point under it.
- **plotmeshGUI: `fscan2d` and `fscan` are read as `motor start step npoints`** (`logfile_reader.read_fullcommand()`). Before, the steps were taken as the last positions and the numbers of points as numbers of intervals, which gave wrong axes.
- **plotmeshGUI: heavy `.h5` logfiles.** Before listing the scans of a logfile of more than 300 scans (about 0.023 s per scan, e.g. 2 min for 5000 scans), a dialog proposes to:
  - open it anyway;
  - list only some scans (filter of `map_workflow.scan_filter()`: words of the dataset name, scan names, prefix, only the scans ended with SUCCESS, number of points, last scans);
  - or open a lighter logfile of the next folders: sample or dataset logfiles, with their number of scans (`logfile_reader.lighter_logfiles()`). Detector files (`eiger4m_####.h5`) and `scanNNNN` folders are not proposed.

### New features
- **plotmeshGUI (`plotmeshspecGUI`), BLISS `.h5` logfiles:**
  - *1D scans*: clicking the *ASCAN* item lists the 1D scans (`ascan`, `dscan`, `a2scan`, `a#scan`, `fscan`). A counter is plotted against the motor; a menu chooses the motor of scans with several motors (`a#scan`).
  - Scans coloured by their end: green for `SUCCESS`, orange for `USER_ABORT`, red otherwise, in the lists of the 2D and 1D scans. The mouse over a scan shows its start date and duration.
  - The selected counter is kept when another scan is selected. When the new scan does not have it, the menu shows its name (and no longer becomes blank). The menu is as wide as the longest counter name.
  - Monitor counter for the normalization found whatever its name (`mon`, `i0`, `io`, also in longer names such as `ct2_mon`), and the exposure time from the scan command (`logfile_reader.find_monitor_counter()`).
  - Titles of the plots: scan, BLISS command, selected counter and, in smaller letters, the scan folder from `RAW_DATA` (`logfile_reader.scan_folder()`, `path_from_raw_data()`).
  - One colour range slider with two handles (`GUI/rangeslider.py`) instead of two sliders. Tick values are rounded.
  - The lists of the 2D and 1D scans are separated by a movable bar, and their width fits the scan names and the screen.
  - *Counters maps* toggle button: in place of the 1D plot, maps of all the counters of the selected 2D scan whose name starts with `fluo`, `eiger4m_` or `xia_sum_fluo` (prefixes editable), by pages of 4, 9 or 16 maps depending on the screen.
    - Title: scan, BLISS command and scan folder. Positions in microns on the left column and the bottom row.
    - The mouse over a map gives the point, the image index and the value. Clicking a map selects its counter in the main plot.
    - *Save figure* (png, pdf, svg): the default folder is the mirror in `PROCESSED_DATA` of the scan folder of `RAW_DATA`, created when needed (and removed if nothing is saved in it).
- **show2DmapGUI replots the results files of mosaic** (`show2DmapGUI [file or folder ...]`, PeakSearchGUI *Mosaic & Monitor*):
  - `roicounters_*.h5` of the parallel ROI counters workflow, with a choice of counters (`plot_counters_results()`);
  - text maps (`<counter>_2D_*`, `MOSAIC_image_Plot_*`). They now start with a `# key: value` header: data type, quantity, image indices, map and box sizes, ROI centre, images folder, detector and title. `np.loadtxt` skips it. Files without header (written before) are recognized by their name, and their image indices are read from the 1D file of the same date;
  - the map windows have *File/Save Data* (text map with header) and *File/Open Data* (any results file);
  - without wx: `mosaic.read_map_file()`, `read_counters_results()`, `list_map_files()`, `show_map_file()`.

### Fixes
- **`plotmeshGUI` command:** it pointed to `LaueTools.plotmeshspecGUI:start`, which does not exist, and failed after `pip install`. It now starts `LaueTools.GUI.plotmeshspecGUI`. `pyperclip`, imported by plotmeshGUI, is now a dependency.
- **`matchingrate` can be imported on its own.** Importing it first failed (circular import `matchingrate` → `indexingSpotsSet` → `indexingAnglesLUT` → `matchingrate`).
- **plotmeshGUI:**
  - Logfiles without mesh scan gave an `IndexError`.
  - With numpy 2, comparisons of arrays with `[]` raised a `ValueError`.
  - Aborted scans (fewer points than in the command) failed when a counter had one value less than the others. The missing points are drawn as zeros, and the normalization by the monitor uses the points common to the counter and the monitor.
  - The normalization failed with a `KeyError` when the logfile had no `integration_time`.

### Tests
- `tests/test_imports.py`: import of the main modules; each command of `pyproject.toml` points to an existing function; `matchingrate` imported alone in a new interpreter.
- `tests/test_logfile_reader.py`: synthetic BLISS `RAW_DATA` tree (proposal → sample → dataset logfiles). Covers the `fscan2d`, `fscan` and `amesh` commands, the monitor and image counters, lighter logfiles, logfiles without mesh, the 1D scans and their motors, and the scan folders.
- `tests/test_mosaic_mapfiles.py`: text maps with and without header, `roicounters` `.h5` files.
- `tests/test_indexfileseries.py` removed (placeholder test).

### Packaging
- **New command:** `show2DmapGUI` (`LaueTools.show2DmapGUI:start`).
- **New dependency:** `pyperclip` (also in `conda_requirements.txt`).
- **Excluded from the package:** `parabolmirror.py` (a ray tracing study of mirrors, not a LaueTools module).
